Explore nature. Keep a record. Share what you find.

BioSfere is a citizen science platform for recording and sharing observations of living organisms with photos, locations and scientific classification. It brings field observations of plants, animals and fungi together in one place.

To create an observation, add your own photo, select the location and enter the scientific classification you know. Share organisms you have not identified yet and explore records by species and location.

Photo-based species suggestions can help you begin an identification. Check suggestions against the organism’s distinguishing features and the observation conditions; a suggestion is not a confirmed identification.

Create an account to share observations, keep your own field records and contribute to a shared biodiversity map. BioSfere is a place for nature enthusiasts and biology students to learn together. The application supports Turkish and English.

What can you do on BioSfere?

  • Share nature observations with photos
  • Explore a shared observation map
  • Search species and organize scientific classification
  • Use photo-based species suggestions
  • Learn with other nature observers

How to share your first nature observation

  1. Choose a photo you took. Start with a photo that shows the organism’s overall appearance and distinguishing features. Avoid disturbing wildlife or damaging its habitat.
  2. Record the date and setting. Note where and when you observed it. Take care when sharing sensitive locations, such as nesting or breeding sites.
  3. Add the identification you know. Keep uncertainty visible if you are unsure. Treat photo-based suggestions as a starting point to check, rather than a definitive identification.
  4. Create and share your record. Use your free account to bring the photo, notes and observation details together. Add what you learn later.
